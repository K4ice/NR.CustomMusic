[size=5][b]Custom Music[/b][/size]
Add your own music to the in-game music player.

Drop [b].mp3 / .ogg / .wav[/b] files into a folder — they play in the garage, on the streets, at meetspots and in races, right alongside the original soundtrack. Skipping, crossfades, shuffle and the [i]Now Playing[/i] popup all work as with the original tracks.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Your tracks become real tracks of the in-game player
[*]Choose playlists: Cruise, Meetspot, Garage, Race, RaceWin, RaceLoss, MainMenu, Loading
[*]Optional: replace the original soundtrack completely
[*]Tracks are kept compressed in memory — big playlists are fine
[*]No game files are modified
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Game version [b]3.1.9[/b] or [b]3.1.11[/b]
[*][url=https://builds.bepinex.dev/projects/bepinex_be]BepInEx 6 IL2CPP x64[/url], build be.788 or newer
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install BepInEx: unzip it into the game folder (next to [i]NIGHT-RUNNERS PRIVATE ALPHA.exe[/i]) and launch the game once.
[*]Unzip this mod into the game folder.
[*]Put your music into the [b]CustomMusic[/b] folder.
[*]Launch the game.
[/list]

Track names come from file names. The original soundtrack uses [i]title - artist[/i] — name your files the same way for a consistent look.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[i]BepInEx/config/nr.custommusic.cfg[/i] (created after the first launch):
[list]
[*][b]MusicFolder[/b] — folder with your tracks (default: CustomMusic)
[*][b]Playlists[/b] — which playlists get your tracks (default: Cruise,Meetspot,Garage,Race)
[*][b]ReplaceOriginal[/b] — true = only your tracks play
[*][b]Weight[/b] — shuffle chance (original tracks use 1)
[/list]

[size=4][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]
Check [i]BepInEx/LogOutput.log[/i] for lines with [b][Custom Music][/b]. If you report a bug, please attach this file.
